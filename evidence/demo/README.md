- The presenter depicts Adhel with his consent and is AI-generated. Bram is a
  synthetic preset voice, not a clone of Adhel's voice. Higgsfield was used for
  media production only; it is not part of Relay's execution engine.
- The browser footage records real local replay of the saved discovery artifact:
  successful preparation, a missing-member business outcome, and a paused
  session restored through the operator controls.
- The recorded handoff was operated by an AI assistant, not a physical human.
  It demonstrates the same-session control-transfer mechanism, not human identity.
- Bank panels in the workbench are masked checkpoint snapshots, not a live video
  feed. All bank data is synthetic. The workflow stops at review; no account is
  created.
- Diagrams and tool slides are explanatory graphics. No new model discovery run
  is shown in the video. Consult `../submission/discovery/` for discovery evidence.
- Footage includes cuts and held frames for narration. It is not an uninterrupted
  run or a performance benchmark.
